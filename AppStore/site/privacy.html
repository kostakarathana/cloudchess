Privacy policy

Your game stays on your device

CloudChess is developed by Kosta Karathanasopoulos. Puzzle generation, chess analysis and adaptive coaching run on your device. Progress, scores, preferences, collected styles and coaching estimates are stored locally. The app has no advertising or analytics SDK and does not send gameplay or coaching records to a CloudChess server.

Optional profile imports

If you request a Chess.com or Lichess profile import, the app sends the username you enter directly to the selected provider over HTTPS to download public games. That provider receives the network request, including your IP address, and handles it under its own privacy policy. CloudChess does not ask for your provider password or sign you into that account. Downloaded games and the resulting analysis are stored and processed on your device.

Storage and removal

Local records remain until removed with the app. To remove CloudChess's local data, use Delete App in iOS Settings; Offload App retains data. Deleting CloudChess does not delete your Chess.com or Lichess account or its public games. Device backups may contain app data according to your Apple backup settings; manage those backups in your device settings.

Support and external pages

When you email support, we receive the address and information you choose to send and use them to respond to your request. Do not include passwords. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address. Opening an external link contacts its website. Our public policy and support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process standard request information under its privacy statement. The pages do not contain our own analytics or tracking cookies.

Questions and updates

Contact kostakarathanasopoulos@proton.me with privacy questions. Changes to these practices will be reflected in this policy and its updated date.
