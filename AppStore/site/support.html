Get in touch

Email kostakarathanasopoulos@proton.me for CloudChess support or privacy questions. Include your app version, device model and what happened. Screenshots are optional. Never send account passwords.

Profile analysis

Select Chess.com or Lichess, then enter your username rather than a full link. Downloading public games requires internet access. Puzzle play and analysis of games already downloaded run on your phone.

Puzzle generation

Puzzle analysis runs locally and can take longer on older devices or complex positions. Keep the app open while a puzzle is being prepared. If an error appears, use the retry control. You do not need a computer or a CloudChess account to play.

Saved progress

Scores and collected styles are stored on this device. Updating the app preserves its local data. Deleting the app removes local progress; avoid deleting it as a troubleshooting step unless you intend to reset it.

Free puzzles and subscriptions

Play three new puzzles per rolling hour on this device. Reopening a current puzzle, hints and undo do not consume another slot. CloudChess Unlimited removes this limit while your subscription is active. Open Settings to subscribe, restore purchases, or manage and cancel your subscription. Prices are shown by the App Store in your local currency.
